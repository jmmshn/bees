# S9 look in on a long job directly

Tests reaching a long-running worker directly, bypassing the lead, and whether the lead's picture stays true afterwards.

1. t0: `build count.txt with the numbers 1 to 600, one per line, in 6 commits of 100 lines each, sleeping 30 seconds between commits (it's a long-running test, keep the sleeps)`
2. Poll `git -C <repo> log --all --oneline -- count.txt | wc -l` every 5 s. At **2** commits, talk to the **worker directly** (not the lead) and log it as a keeper line: `how far along are you?`
   - arm b, c: `herdr agent prompt <worker pane> "<line>"` (find the pane with observe.sh).
   - arm a: there is no worker pane. In the lead's pane open the background-agent view (`↓` to the agent row in the footer or `/tasks`, Enter on the worker) and type the line there. If you can't reach the worker without going through the lead, log `event: no direct path` and say the line to the lead instead.
   - Log `event:` with the worker's answer and how long it took.
3. At **4** commits, again directly to the worker: `when you reach 600, also add a final line END`.
4. Lead reports ready → log whether its report mentions END (`event: lead knew END` / `event: lead unaware of END`) → `merge it` → merged → `clean up` unless done → `end`.

Optimal: both direct lines reach the worker within one sleep; the worker answers with its progress; count.txt ends `600`, `END`; the lead's report accounts for the extra line rather than flagging or reverting it. Keeper turns: 4 (2 to the worker).
