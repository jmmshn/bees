# S7 lead restart

Tests whether work and state survive the lead dying.

1. t0: `build count.txt with the numbers 1 to 500, one per line, in 5 commits of 100 lines each, sleeping 30 seconds between commits (it's a durability test, keep the sleeps)`
2. Poll `git -C <repo> log --all --oneline -- count.txt` every 10 s. When it shows 2 commits: log `event: killing lead`, kill the lead's process (`kill <pid>`, pid from `~/.claude/sessions/*.json` with `.name == <lead>`), wait for its pane to return to a shell, then relaunch it in the same pane with `herdr pane run <pane> "<cmd>"` (intervention):
   - arm a: `claude --continue --name <lead> --append-system-prompt-file <bees>/bakeoff/arms/A-subagents.md`
   - arm b: `claude --continue --name <lead> --append-system-prompt-file <bees>/bakeoff/arms/B-peers.md`
   - arm c: `bs queen`
3. When the lead is idle again → `status?` — log what it knows (does it know about the worker, its branch, progress?).
4. If the work finished (5 commits) and the lead reports it ready → `merge it` → merged → `clean up` unless done → `end`. If the work died with the lead, log `event: work lost`, say `please finish it` once, and continue.

Optimal: the worker keeps going through the restart, the new lead finds it and its progress unprompted beyond `status?`, merge with 5 commits. Keeper turns: 3.
