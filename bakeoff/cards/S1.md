# S1 single fix

Tests the fixed overhead of one delegation and the merge-and-cleanup ritual.

1. t0: `fix the typos in poem.txt`
2. Lead reports the work ready (or done) → `merge it`
3. Merged (by the lead or by your `!` line, then `merged`) → if the lead hasn't said its workers and branches are cleaned up: `clean up` .
4. Lead confirms cleanup → `end`.

Optimal: lead delegates one worker → worker fixes feild, yeild, retrun and pushes its branch → lead checks the diff and reports ready → merge → lead ends the worker and removes its branch. Keeper turns: 2.
