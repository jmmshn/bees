# S12 mail to a worker that is down

From crew: nudges and answers sent to busy, finished or dead sessions were lost or collided (crew c623649, ad02498; bees "Done when" 3). Tests whether a message to a stopped worker survives until it runs again.

1. t0: `build count.txt with the numbers 1 to 500, one per line, in 5 commits of 100 lines each, sleeping 30 seconds between commits (it's a durability test, keep the sleeps)`
2. At **2** commits (poll `git -C <repo> log --all --oneline -- count.txt | wc -l`), stop the worker (intervention), as in S11: kill its claude pid (arm b, c) or stop it from the lead's background-agent view (arm a; if impossible, `stop the worker now` to the lead). Log `event: worker down`.
3. To the lead: `message the worker: when you finish, add a final line END after 500`. Log `event:` with what the lead says happened to the message (sent, queued, failed, nothing).
4. To the lead: `now restart the worker and let it finish`.
5. Lead reports ready → log whether END is there → `merge it` → merged → `clean up` unless done → `end`.

Optimal: the message is stored while the worker is down and delivered when it restarts, without the lead re-sending it from memory; count.txt is 1..500 then END; the first two commits survive. Captain turns: 4.
