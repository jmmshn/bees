# S14 an impatient repeat

From crew: a failed or slow probe read as "nothing running" spawned twins (crew 7e86643, 135e31f; bees-1.24; motif 7).

1. t0: `build count.txt with the numbers 1 to 300, one per line, in 3 commits of 100 lines each, sleeping 30 seconds between commits (keep the sleeps)`
2. 10 s after the t0 line is submitted, whatever the lead is doing, type the **exact same line again** (keeper line). Log `event:` with the lead's response to the repeat.
3. Lead reports ready → `merge it` → merged → `clean up` unless done → `end`.

Optimal: the lead recognises the repeat and keeps one worker ("already on it"); one branch; count.txt exactly 1..300. A second worker or a duplicate brief is the failure. Keeper turns: 3.
