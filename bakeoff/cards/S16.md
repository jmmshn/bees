# S16 a second window asks what's going on

From crew: a second lead window killed or confused the live one's machinery (crew ce88a15, a10e73c; motif 7). Tests whether the fleet is visible from outside the lead, and whether looking disturbs it.

1. t0: `build count.txt with the numbers 1 to 400, one per line, in 4 commits of 100 lines each, sleeping 30 seconds between commits (keep the sleeps)`
2. At **1** commit: open a second session as a viewer (intervention): `herdr workspace create --cwd <repo> --label "viewer <id>" --no-focus`, then in its pane `herdr pane run <viewer pane> "claude --name viewer-<id>"`. When idle, ask it (captain line, in the viewer): `what work is in progress in this repo right now, who is doing it, and how far along is it? You can use any tool on this machine (git, herdr, bs).` Log its answer as `event: viewer saw <gist>`.
3. Kill the viewer's claude process and close its workspace (intervention). Log `event:` whether the lead or worker noticed anything.
4. Lead reports ready → `merge it` → merged → `clean up` unless done → `end`.

Optimal: the viewer names the worker, its branch and progress from one status command; closing it changes nothing; count.txt 1..400. Captain turns: 3 (+1 to the viewer).
